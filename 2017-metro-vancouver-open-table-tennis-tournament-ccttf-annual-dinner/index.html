2017 Metro Vancouver Open Table Tennis Tournament & CCTTF Annual Dinner

With the continuous encouragement and support from our board members, honorary presidents and vice presidents, and major sponsors (Richmond Olympic Oval and Continental Seafood Restaurant), once again, we are delighted to co-host the 2017 Metro Vancouver Open Table Tennis Tournament together with Citius Sport Ltd. on September 30 & October 1, 2017, at Richmond Olympic Oval. Taking this opportunity, we are also hosting our 2017 CCTTF Annual Dinner on October 7, 2017 at the Continental Seafood Restaurant (a fun-filled seafood feast with lots of prizes, music and entertainment).

Our Federation has successfully hosted the Metro Vancouver Open & Invitational Table Tennis Tournaments from 2010 to 2013. We had a very exciting weekend of competition with participating teams from our community, media friends and various levels of government agencies.

With the continuous encouragement and support from our board members, honorary presidents and vice presidents, and major sponsors (Richmond Olympic Oval and Continental Seafood Restaurant), once again, we are delighted to co-host the 2017 Metro Vancouver Open Table Tennis Tournament together with Citius Sport Ltd. on September 30 & October 1, 2017, at Richmond Olympic Oval.

Taking this opportunity, we are also hosting our  2017 CCTTF Annual Dinner on October 7, 2017 at the Continental Seafood Restaurant (a fun-filled seafood feast with lots of prizes, music and entertainment).

For details, please refer to the enclosed flyers. If you require further information, please feel free to contact Cindy at 604.773.9986 or Josephine at 604.273.1419.

We look forward to seeing you in our events!
